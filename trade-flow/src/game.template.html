How to play

A treemap shows the trade composition of one of the world's 120 largest economies. Work out which country it is in 6 guesses.

The banner above the treemap tells you whether you are looking at that country's exports (what it sells) or its imports (what it buys). Both flows appear — always check the label first.

After each guess

  • Distance — how far your guess is from the answer.
  • Arrow — the compass direction from your guess toward the answer.
  • Proximity — 100% means you found it.

A new puzzle appears every day. Over 240 days every country shows up once with its exports and once with its imports.

Data

Treemaps are live embeds from the Observatory of Economic Complexity (HS92 classification). The country list is the top 120 economies by World Bank GDP in current US$. See the README for full sourcing.
