How to play

A treemap shows the trade of one of the world's largest economies — every economy with more than a million people. Work out which country it is in 6 guesses.

The banner above the treemap says which of four charts you are looking at, so always read it first:

  • Exports by product — what it sells.
  • Imports by product — what it buys.
  • Exports by destination — who it sells to.
  • Imports by origin — who it buys from.

After each guess

  • Distance — how far your guess is from the answer.
  • Arrow — the compass direction from your guess toward the answer.
  • Proximity — 100% means you found it.

A new puzzle appears every day. Over days every country shows up once with each of its four charts, and nothing repeats inside that window.

Data

Treemaps are live embeds from the Observatory of Economic Complexity (HS92 classification). Countries are ranked by World Bank GDP in current US$. See the README for full sourcing.
